How the program is structured

The original program runs five weeks. For the U.S. edition I am planning adaptations that fit it into roughly three weeks - exactly the same number of sessions, concentrated, rather than spread over a longer period.

And the sessions are about 20% of the program. The other 80% is the work itself: drills, build exercises and assignments that each participant applies to their own business - guided by us and structured, not "go practice on your own".

1

Before we start - personal setup

We set up each participant's working environment and build their first agent with them. Nobody arrives at the first session facing a blank screen.

2

The build sessions

Intensive sessions where we build together. Not lectures - work. Each person advances on their own system, with close support.

3

Assignments and drills - step by step

Between sessions there are drills that take each participant step by step toward their own system: deep work with Claude Code, and a gradual understanding of how you build an agent, how you build a complete system, and how you build an AI office you can keep expanding over time.

4

Between sessions - you are not on your own

Sharpening sessions for questions and consultation, plus the Aimprove AI agent team available for help at any stage. Anyone who gets stuck does not wait for the next session.

5

Afterward - everything stays

The recordings, the learning-materials site, and everything that was built. With no time limit. And most importantly - the method stays, and with it the ability to build any agent, system or automated process you can imagine. Any business problem that comes up, you can build an end-to-end solution for it, quickly and easily, and one that keeps working over time.
